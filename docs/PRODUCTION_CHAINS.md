# Production Chains Reference

Complete guide to all production chains in Medieval Trading Dynasty.

## 🌾 FOOD PRODUCTION CHAINS

### Basic Bread Chain
```
TIER 1: Farm (3 workers, 200g cost)
└─ Produces: 50 Grain/week

TIER 2: Mill (2 workers, 300g cost)
├─ Inputs: 40 Grain/week
└─ Outputs: 40 Flour/week

TIER 3: Bakery (2 workers, 250g cost)
├─ Inputs: 30 Flour/week
└─ Outputs: 60 Bread/week (2:1 ratio!)

PROFIT ANALYSIS:
- Raw grain value: 3g/unit
- Flour value: 5g/unit
- Bread value: 4g/unit
- Full chain: 3g → 240g final value (8x multiplier!)
```

### Beer Production Chain
```
TIER 1: Farm (Grain) + Hops Field
├─ Farm: 50 Grain/week
└─ Hops Field: 20 Hops/week

TIER 2: Brewery (3 workers, 350g cost)
├─ Inputs: 30 Grain + 10 Hops/week
└─ Outputs: 50 Beer/week

PROFIT ANALYSIS:
- Input cost: 30×3g + 10×6g = 150g
- Output value: 50×5g = 250g
- Profit: 100g/week (67% margin)
```

### Wine Production Chain
```
TIER 1: Vineyard (4 workers, 400g cost)
└─ Produces: 40 Grapes/week

TIER 2: Wine Press (3 workers, 450g cost)
├─ Inputs: 30 Grapes/week
└─ Outputs: 30 Wine/week

TIER 3: Luxury Wine (requires Spices)
├─ Inputs: 20 Wine + 5 Spices/week
└─ Outputs: 15 Spiced Wine/week (60g/unit!)

REGIONAL SPECIALTIES:
- Bordeaux: +20% wine quality
- Burgundy: +30% wine value (prestige)
- Rhine: +15% production
```

## 🎯 STRATEGIC CHAIN CONTROL

### Monopoly Opportunities

**Vertical Integration Benefits**:
- Control entire chain = maximum profit
- No dependence on suppliers (price stability)
- Can weather market crashes
- Economies of scale

**Example: Beer Monopoly in Bavaria**
```
OWN:
- 5 Farms (grain production)
- 3 Breweries (beer production)
- 2 Inns (direct sales to customers)
- Warehouses in München, Augsburg, Regensburg

RESULT:
- Control 60% of Bavarian beer market
- Set prices (within reason)
- Crush competitors by price war
- Total profit: 1500g/week
```

### Chokepoint Control

**Control Critical Resources**:
- All salt mines in Austria → every food producer needs you
- All tin mines in Cornwall → control bronze production
- All charcoal kilns near iron forges → smelters depend on you

**Strategic Tax**:
- Don't monopolize (authorities will intervene)
- Control 40-50% of critical resource
- Charge fair price + 10-15% premium
- Competitors can't undercut (you control supply)

## 🚀 BEGINNER'S PROGRESSION PATH

### Phase 1: Manual Trading (Turns 1-50)
- No buildings yet
- Buy low, sell high manually
- Learn price fluctuations
- Build capital to 500g

### Phase 2: First Building (Turns 50-100)
- **Recommended**: Brewery in your home town
- Why? Quick profit, local market, simple
- Revenue: 100-150g/week
- Save profits for expansion

### Phase 3: Vertical Integration (Turns 100-200)
- Add Farm (supply your brewery)
- Now own 2 steps of chain
- Profit increases 50%
- More stable (not dependent on grain market)

### Phase 4: Horizontal Expansion (Turns 200-500)
- Build 2-3 more breweries in nearby cities
- Leverage your grain supply
- Economies of scale
- Profit: 500+g/week

### Phase 5: Empire (Turns 500+)
- Own multiple complete chains
- Cross-regional operations
- Marry into foreign families for access
- Diversify into luxury goods
- Profit: 2000+g/week

## ⚠️ CHAIN VULNERABILITIES

### What Can Break Your Chain?

**1. Supply Disruption**
- War blocks trade route → can't get raw materials
- Bad harvest → grain shortage → brewery stops
- Rival buys all charcoal → forge can't smelt

**Solution**: Stockpile 4-8 weeks of inputs in warehouses

**2. Demand Crash**
- Good harvest everywhere → bread prices crash
- Plague kills population → luxury goods demand dies
- Peace treaty → weapon prices collapse

**Solution**: Diversify (don't put all gold in one chain)

**3. Competition**
- Rival builds 5 breweries → beer market saturated
- Imports from cheaper region flood market
- Guild restricts your sales

**Solution**: Either price war OR pivot to new market

**4. Regulatory**
- New tariff makes import unprofitable
- Guild membership revoked
- Duke confiscates your mine

**Solution**: Political connections (marry into nobility)
